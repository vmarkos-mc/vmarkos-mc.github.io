Contact

Contact Options

Assuming you need for some (important or not) reason to contact me, by far the most preferred way is via email, at: vmarkos@mc-class.gr.

An almost equally good alternative is using your college MS Teams account, through which you can message me directly - my MS Teams responses times, nevertheless, tend to be longer.

We might also just happen to encounter each other at the College Campus, in which case, feel free to contact me directly.

Contact Reasons

You might contact me for anything related to your studies and, in at least one way, to a module we enjoy (or not) together. Such examples include but do not restrict to:

  • Questions about modules, in particular module assessment.
  • Inquiries about meeting slots outside office hours - since office hours do not, in practice, work for all equally well.
  • Requests for thesis supervision - you can also find research areas I am interested into here.
  • Issues you would like to discuss with me, somehow related to your studies - e.g., what’s next?

While, in general, I am quite open to discussion, there are some things I will most probably have no or surprisingly useless feedback to provide. Those include but do not restrict to:

  • General administration - refer directly to the appropriate department of our secretariat.
  • Procedures and stuff about modules I do not deliver - even I might have delivered them in the past, things do change in life.
  • Astrology.

Will You Ever Receive A Reply?

Yes, and you should expect it in most cases within 24 hours, excluding weekends. Beyond that point, even if I might be swamping due to other duties or escaping to some tax heaven, feel free to send me a reminder, if your matter is urgent!
